Top Attractions in Valparai

Aliyar Dam

Scenic reservoir at the foot of the ghats.

Monkey Falls

A roadside waterfall on the climb up.

Nirar Dam & Tea Estates

Endless tea gardens and viewpoints.

Sholayar Dam

One of Asia’s deepest dams amid forest.

Loam’s View Point

Panoramic plains and ghat view.

Anamalai Tiger Reserve

Wildlife including elephants and lion-tailed macaques.
