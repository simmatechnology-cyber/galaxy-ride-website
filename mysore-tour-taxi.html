Top Attractions in Mysore

Mysore Palace

Indo-Saracenic royal palace, dazzling when lit.

Chamundi Hills

Hilltop temple and the giant Nandi statue.

Brindavan Gardens

Musical fountain and terraced gardens at KRS dam.

St. Philomena’s Church

One of India’s tallest neo-Gothic churches.

Mysore Zoo

One of the oldest, best-kept zoos in India.

Devaraja Market

Vibrant bazaar for flowers, incense and silk.
