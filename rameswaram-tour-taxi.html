Top Attractions in Rameswaram

Ramanathaswamy Temple

India’s longest temple corridor (1,212m).

Agnitheertham

Sacred sea-shore bathing ghat.

Pamban Bridge

Iconic rail-and-road sea bridge.

Dhanushkodi

Ghost town at the tip where two seas meet.

Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Memorial

Tribute to the Missile Man at his birthplace.

Kothandaramaswamy Temple

Lone temple that survived the 1964 cyclone.
