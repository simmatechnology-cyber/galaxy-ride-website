Top Attractions in Kanyakumari

Vivekananda Rock Memorial

Island shrine reached by ferry.

Thiruvalluvar Statue

133-ft monument to the Tamil poet-saint.

Sunrise & Sunset Point

See both over the confluence of three seas.

Kumari Amman Temple

Ancient seashore temple to the Goddess.

Gandhi Mandapam

Memorial built where Gandhi’s ashes were kept.

Thanumalayan Temple

Historic temple at nearby Suchindram.
